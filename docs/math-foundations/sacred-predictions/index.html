Verification Criteria​

A prediction is VERIFIED if:

  1. Experimental measurement exists
  2. Measured value falls within uncertainty bounds
  3. Measurement is from a reputable source (CODATA, PDG, peer-reviewed journal)

A prediction is FALSIFIED if:

  1. Experimental measurement exists
  2. Measured value falls outside uncertainty bounds
  3. Measurement is from a reputable source
