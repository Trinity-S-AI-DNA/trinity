PROVENANCE -- apps/website/public/t27f/gnn/
=============================================

What this page is
-----------------
"Graph neural networks, gently" -- a deliberately improved rebuild of the 2021
Distill introduction to graph neural networks, running entirely in the browser
with zero dependencies: no tfjs, no CDN, no framework. Three files:
index.html (prose + chrome), gnn.js (engine + figures, ~800 lines), karate.js
(embedded dataset). ASCII source. No network fetches beyond the site's own
fonts, which every t27f page already loads.

Verification (run headless in Node, 2026-10-25)
-----------------------------------------------
selfTest() in gnn.js, green on the shipped head:
- gradient check vs finite differences, relative error 3.3e-10;
- karate default config (2 layers, sum, mp on) trains to accuracy 1.000 in
  300 epochs; mp-off control lands at 0.588;
- permutation invariance on the fixture: max |logit delta| 8.9e-16 (float
  rounding, as the page states);
- karate.js self-check: 78 edges, 17/17 labels, every node in one faction.
DOM smoke test (stub DOM in Node): all nine figure containers populate, the
24-model sweep completes, the playground trains to 600 epochs, pauses, and
reports no NaN. Not verified: pixel layout in a real browser (no browser was
connected on this machine during the build).

Honest deviations stated on the page
------------------------------------
- The in-page sweep reproduces two of the 2021 design lessons (depth plateaus
  at 2-3 layers and drops at 4; message passing on beats off by ~0.32 acc) but
  NOT the aggregation ranking: on karate with this engine, mean and max edge
  out sum. The page says so and explains why (unnormalized sums saturate tanh).
- The playground is transductive (train = test) on one 34-node dataset; the
  page labels it a reproduction at toy scale, not a benchmark.
