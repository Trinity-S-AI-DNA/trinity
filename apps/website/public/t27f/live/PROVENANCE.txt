/t27f/live/ -- where every byte on the page comes from
======================================================

The T27F live surface (issue Trinity-S-AI-DNA/trinity#1717, milestone M3 of T27F Live,
t27#10262). The page replays a recorded log of actor events through the law compiled to
WebAssembly. It holds no HUD, no layout, no colour and no replay state of its own:

  live.js                the loader (21 lines, comments included): fetch the recorded log,
                         import the glue, hand it the canvas and the log, print the status.
                         FR-004: no layout, no colour, no state logic. When the glue or the
                         wasm is missing it catches the failure and names the state.
  logs/recorded_log.json the recorded log, format t27f-live-recorded-log-v1, 24 events.

The law (in gHashTag/t27, compiled to t27f.wasm -- none of it is vendored here):

  work_graph.t27         the work graph the events land in     master 164705e28f
  hud.t27                the HUD, a pure function of the graph master ed6dcf00af
  live_mode.t27          the frame clock and the replay        master b086799248
  display_list.t27       the ops the graph and HUD emit        master e356cfb589
  live_stream.t27        the event stream contract             master d800c56d1a
  scene.t27              the scene the ops paint into          master e356cfb589
  motion.t27             the motion between frames             master e356cfb589

(gHashTag/t27 master commit shas, the last commit that touched each file, read from the
GitHub API on 2026-10-24.)

The recorded log
  Extracted deterministically from specs/t27f/work_graph_fixture.t27 KIND[0..23] at
  gHashTag/t27 master 50ea972f7e. Verified against the fixture's own
  REPLAY_KIND_REF : [24]u8 = [0,0,0,0,0,0,0,4,4,4,4,4,4,4,2,3,1,4,5,4,4,4,5,2]:
  0=spawn x7, 4=deliver x7, 2=down, 3=restart, 1=exit, then deliver/delivers to the
  final down at tick 23. The log's 24 events match that sequence one for one.

The state of the wasm: PENDING (2026-10-24)
  t27f.wasm and its glue t27f_live.js are NOT in this directory yet. The page ships with
  the loader and the log; the status line names the dark state until the module lands.
  A loader without its wasm is a page that renders its chrome and names its state --
  never a fake replay.

  What is already proven (t27#1717 comment, 2026-10-23): the wasm32 blocker is fixed

  built on the lab, never on the owner's Mac.
