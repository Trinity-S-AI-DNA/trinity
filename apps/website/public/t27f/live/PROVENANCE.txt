/t27f/live/ -- where every byte on the page comes from
=======================================================

The T27F live page (Milestone M3 of T27F Live: t27#10262, hud + a11y_mirror;
M4 discipline: t27#11778 live_mode). The page replays a recorded agent log.

  live.js (10 lines) loads t27f.wasm and logs/recorded_log.json and drives the
  shared glue (t27f_live.js). It holds no layout, colour or state logic (FR-004).
  t27f_live.js (~30 lines) moves bytes in and frames out; it decides nothing.

Surface status on 2026-10-23
  - The loader and page chrome land here.
  - t27f.wasm is reused from /t27f/graph/ (reel + deck + spec_graph_* modules).
    The HUD layer (hud.t27, a11y_mirror.t27, live_mode.t27, work_graph, scene)
    is NOT in this wasm yet: those specs live on t27 PR #11782 (open) and need a
    lab rebuild of export.zig. Until the lab is reachable (t27os#3, "Lab
    restoration"), the stage shows the recorded log's agent/state text.
  - recorded_log.json is a 5-frame sample log; replace with a real recorded run.
