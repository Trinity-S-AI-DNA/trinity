/t27f/graph/ -- where every byte on the page comes from
=======================================================

The T27F graph view (epic gHashTag/trinity#1648, RFC gHashTag/trinity#1649). The page draws one
box per node and one line per edge of a .t27 spec. It holds no graph, no layout and no verdict
of its own:

  spec_graph_export.t27   reads the spec's bytes into the graph (exact nodes and edges only;
                          at most 192 nodes and 254 edges; over that it refuses, never truncates)
  spec_graph_view.t27     places, sizes and colours every box and line
  spec_graph.t27          the graph IR both of them share

Both run as t27f.wasm. graph.js (39 lines, comments included) is the whole of the hand-written
JavaScript: it loads the wasm, writes the spec's bytes in, loops over nodes and edges and emits
SVG. The one number it computes is how many characters of "kind name" fit in a box's first line
(font size x 0.6); the full text is in the box's tooltip.

Reproduced on the Railway lab on 2026-10-18 from these vendored specs:
  t27c gen-js spec_graph_view.t27 gives spec_graph_view.mjs byte for byte;
  t27c gen spec_graph_view.t27 and spec_graph_export.t27 give the two .zig above;
  zig build-exe export.zig -target wasm32-freestanding -fno-entry -rdynamic -O ReleaseSmall
  -femit-bin=t27f.wasm gives t27f.wasm byte for byte.
(Generate from a directory laid out as specs/t27f/, so that the `use t27f::...` lines resolve.)
